Event Setup Planner FAQs

Use a Quick setup or build your own. Adjust the layout, save a draft, and request a quote with your equipment list and planner image.

Can I plan my event with Events Rental Hub?+

Absolutely. Start with a Quick setup or use Custom setup, then add equipment, move items on the canvas, and review your list before saving or requesting a quote.

Can I rent tents, tables and chairs?+

Absolutely. Pick a ready-made Quick setup or add Single items one at a time. Popular rental equipment you can include is:

  • White Retractable Tent Rental
  • Lifetime Table Rental (4FT and 6FT)
  • Monobloc Chair Rental
  • Iwata Air Cooler Rental
  • Iwata Industrial Fan Rental
  • Gold Stanchion with Red Rope Rental
What does Custom setup do?+

Just enter your guest count, table type, seats per table, and room size. The planner creates a complete seating layout and lets you know when the selected space is too small.

Can I request a quote from this planner?+

Yes. When your layout is ready, select Continue to quote. Your equipment list and planner image move to the catalog quote form. Email RFQs attach the layout PNG when it is small enough. Messenger includes a text summary of the plan, then downloads the PNG so you can attach it in the chat.

Can I save my layout and continue later?+

Yes. Select Save current to keep a draft in this browser on this device. Saved drafts stay local until you select Continue to quote, which is the step that carries the layout into the request for quotation.

Where can I rent tents, tables, and chairs?+

Events Rental Hub provides tent, table, and chair rentals in Manila, Parañaque, Las Piñas, Makati, Mandaluyong, Pasay, Pasig, Quezon City, San Juan, and Taguig in Metro Manila; plus Cavite, Tagaytay, Laguna, and nearby South Luzon areas. Add your exact venue when requesting a quote so availability, access, safety requirements, and final placement can be reviewed.
