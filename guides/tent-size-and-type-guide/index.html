Questions about this guide

Can I choose a tent from guest count alone?+

No. Guest count is one input. Events Rental Hub also reviews the layout, venue space, access, ground condition, and equipment placed under the tent.

Are all tent measurements confirmed online?+

Only published sizes and specifications are shown. Any missing measurement or venue-specific configuration is confirmed in the quotation.
