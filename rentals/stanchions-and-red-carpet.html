Gold stanchions with red rope

Each rope is limited to 3.5FT and cannot be extended beyond that in the planner. For one straight side, allow a post at each end and between every 3.5FT segment.

  • Up to 3.5FT: plan 2 posts
  • Up to 7FT: plan 3 posts
  • Up to 10.5FT: plan 4 posts

These are conceptual quantities for one straight run. Curves, corners, parallel queue sides, entrances, and venue clearances change the final requirement.

Stanchion and Red Carpet Rental FAQs

What red carpet sizes are available?+

The catalog lists 3ft x 10ft and 3ft x 40ft red carpet options. Availability, quantity, and final layout are confirmed in the formal quotation.

What is included with a gold stanchion rental?+

The catalog lists one post and one rope for the available gold stanchion item. Request the required quantity for Events Rental Hub to review availability.

How much are black and silver belt stanchions?+

Black Stanchions (Belt) and Silver Stanchions (Belt) are listed at ₱350 per stanchion. Each unit has a built-in retractable black belt with a listed length of 200cm.

Can stanchions and red carpet be quoted together?+

Yes. Add the requested pieces to the quote request and include your venue, schedule, and intended layout so Events Rental Hub can review the event requirements.
